# Hackathon demo script

Target duration: **3–5 minutes**. This script is a planned walkthrough; the application has not yet been implemented.

## Walkthrough

1. **Problem and beneficiary (about 30–45 seconds):** Introduce MoneyQuest as a way for people navigating important financial decisions to practice choices and learn from consequences in a low-stakes setting. State the Finance impact theme and Financial Resilience starting journey. Do not claim measured long-term impact from the prototype.
2. **Learner journey (about 1.5–2 minutes):** Show the fictional Nigerian unexpected-expense scenario. Make a choice, reveal the deterministic consequence and authored explanation, reflect, then retry or continue to the next challenge. Show the resulting progress.
3. **Component and AI boundary (about 30–45 seconds):** Explain that the Scenario Engine determines the outcome. Show the optional AI Coach explaining that outcome or offering practice without changing it.
4. **Codex’s role (about 45–60 seconds):** Show representative evidence of planning, a code change, focused tests, review, and documentation. Highlight repository instructions and decisions that helped preserve the deterministic boundary.
5. **Adoption and next month (about 30–45 seconds):** Point to setup and scenario-authoring documentation. Describe the next month as an opportunity to expand reviewed Financial Resilience content and validate the learning journey, subject to feedback and the team’s decisions.

## Demo preparation

- Use fictional learner details and illustrative amounts.
- Ensure the complete learning loop is visible and the authored fallback explanation works without the AI Coach.
- Demonstrate that an AI response cannot change the deterministic scenario result.
- Keep a truthful, reviewable Codex work trail available.
- Distinguish intended benefits from measured outcomes; the prototype does not establish long-term impact.
